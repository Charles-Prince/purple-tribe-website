Where we've started

In November 2025, we held the Equipped Youth Conference at Akroma Plaza in Takoradi — 110 young people, including students from Ahantaman Girls SHS and Fijai SHS, in a full day of talks, discussion and mentorship. It's the beginning of what we intend to keep doing, in more schools, for more young people.

ABOUT PURPLE TRIBE

Mission, vision & values.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to raise young people of character and conviction — helping them take their studies, their faith and their future seriously, so they leave school ready to shape the world, not just enter it.

OUR VISION

Our vision is a generation of young people who are not only academically capable, but grounded — who know what they believe, why it matters, and how to live it out.

WHAT WE DO

A day that starts something.

Everything begins with a conference. What follows is what makes it last.

01 · THE MAIN EVENT

Conferences

A full day in a school hall — speaker sessions, performances, honest discussion and mentorship, all built around one theme: taking life seriously. Not just studies, but character, purpose and faith. Students leave with something to think about, and a next step to take.

02 · WHAT COMES NEXT

The Purple Tribe Club

After the conference ends, the conversation doesn't have to. Where a school welcomes it, we help set up a student-led club — run by a student president, guided by a patron teacher, meeting every two weeks to keep working on the same things: study habits, character, purpose, and support.

03 · THE LONG GAME

Mentorship & Beyond

Real relationships that outlast a single event. Students who want to go further are matched with mentors and stay connected through a WhatsApp community — through holidays, exams, and whatever comes after school.
